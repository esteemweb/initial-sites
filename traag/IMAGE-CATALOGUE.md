## Flags for you to decide, not resolved

1. **Duplicate.** 19 files, 18 photographs. (6) and (19) are the same file.
2. **The red tee, (6)/(19).** The palette has four values and red is not one of them. In the hero it is grayscaled in state 1 and duotoned after, so it costs nothing there. Anywhere else it is a fifth colour. I propose hero-only.
3. **The city set is not flash-lit.** (2), (3) and (4) are ambient, long-exposure, warm sodium light. The brief says all 19 share the on-camera-flash look. Her, the work and the room do. The city does not. Two options: use the city set only through the duotone so the treatment unifies it, or accept a second look for three images. I lean to the first.
4. **Sodium orange is not ink.** The city set and (8) carry a warm orange-brown cast that sits near the ink colour without being it. In colour they introduce a tint the palette forbids. Same fix as above.
5. **Mid-grey concrete behind the portraits.** Exactly the case the brief warns about for the threshold state. Blacks will need crushing first, as specced, and I will check the silhouette holds on all five studio portraits before committing to the cycle.
6. **(9) jumps.** Arms up, blur. The brief says a room at 100 BPM leans. (10) leans. If only one crowd shot is used, it should be (10).
7. **Resolution ceiling.** Longest edge is 1536px. A full-bleed image at 1440 on a 2x display will upscale. With this much grain it will hold, but it is worth knowing.
8. **No photograph shows her playing.** (14) shows hands on a deck, face out of frame. Alt text will say "hands", not "her hands".
